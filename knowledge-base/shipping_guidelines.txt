Shipping Guidelines:
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days for domestic orders. Express shipping is available for an additional fee and delivers within 1-3 business days.

International shipping is available to most countries and typically takes 10-15 business days. Additional customs duties and taxes may apply depending on the destination country.

All orders above $50 qualify for free standard shipping within the continental United States. Orders placed before 2 PM EST on business days are processed the same day.

Tracking information is sent via email once your order has been shipped. You can also track your package through our website by entering your order ID on the Order Tracking page.

For large or heavy items, freight shipping may be required. Our team will contact you with a shipping quote before processing these orders. Delivery appointments can be scheduled for freight shipments.
