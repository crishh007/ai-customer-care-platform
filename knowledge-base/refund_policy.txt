Refund Policy Guidelines:
Customers are eligible for a full refund on any unused services or unopened products within 30 days of purchase. To request a refund, please contact our support team with your order ID and receipt.

Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after approval. The refund will be credited back to the original payment method used during purchase.

For digital products or activated subscriptions, refunds are available within 14 days of purchase only if the service has not been substantially used. Usage exceeding 20% of the subscription period will void the refund eligibility.

For items returned in used but acceptable condition, partial refunds may be issued. The refund amount will be determined after inspection by our quality team.

Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to a defective product or an error on our part. Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless otherwise stated.
