Customer Support Hours:
Our customer support team is available Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM EST. We are closed on weekends and major US holidays.

For urgent issues outside business hours, you can reach our emergency support line at 1-800-555-0199. Emergency support is available 24/7 for critical account security issues and service outages.

Live chat support is available during business hours through our website. Average wait times are under 2 minutes during normal hours and under 5 minutes during peak periods.

Email support is available at support@example.com. We aim to respond to all email inquiries within 4 business hours during operating hours.

Self-service options are available 24/7 through our Help Center at help.example.com. Our knowledge base contains answers to frequently asked questions, troubleshooting guides, and video tutorials.
